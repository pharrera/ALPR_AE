A New Home

The Long Beach Accelerator Tech Hub Center

Downtown Long Beach · Opened September 28

The week opened with the unveiling of our new Tech Hub Center, supported by a grant from the U.S. Small Business Administration and the Honorable U.S. Senator Dianne Feinstein.

The Center is a place for thought leaders and key investors to engage with public and private partners, and to grow emerging technology companies and ecosystems.
