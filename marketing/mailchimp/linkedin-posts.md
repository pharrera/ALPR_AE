## Long Beach Tech Week 2026 recap

Pairs with the `2026-10-08-recap` e-blast. Post after the e-blast lands, not
before: the release link is the same in both, and the e-blast should be the
thing that breaks it.

```
Long Beach Tech Week 2026 is a wrap.

Over four days, investors, industry leaders and entrepreneurs came together to
shape what comes next for Southern California's technology economy. We also
opened the doors to the new Long Beach Accelerator Tech Hub Center in downtown
Long Beach, supported by a grant from the U.S. Small Business Administration
and the Honorable U.S. Senator Dianne Feinstein.

Congratulations to our 2026 honorees:

Visionary of the Year: Dr. Noel Hacegaba, CEO, Port of Long Beach
Trailblazer of the Year: Carrie Marshall, CEO & Co-Founder, Rebel Space Technologies
Investor of the Year: Joshua Y. Lee, Managing Partner, Gumshoe Ventures
Founder of the Year: Ethan Glass, CEO, Ocra

Thank you to our planning partners: the City of Long Beach, the Institute for
Innovation and Entrepreneurship at California State University, Long Beach, and
Sunstone Investment Group.

Full release: https://www.lbaccelerator.org/long-beach-accelerator-kicks-off-tech-week-2026/

#LongBeachTechWeek #LongBeachAccelerator
```

**Before posting**, tag the four honorees' personal profiles and the three
partner organisations. That is what carries the post beyond our own followers,
and it is the reason the step-and-repeat portraits are worth pulling.

Four images is the most LinkedIn shows without collapsing the set behind a
"+n" tile, so pick the four that stand alone: the Summit room, the Tech Hub
Center, one award moment, one group shot.
