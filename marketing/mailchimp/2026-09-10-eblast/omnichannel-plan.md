## One finding from the account data

Mailchimp's own analysis of the account, run as part of building this plan:

- Click rate **1.504%**, against a peer median of **1.498%** — on par.
- List churn **42.539%**, against a peer median of **13.313%** — roughly three times the median.

On a list under 1,000, churn at that level is worth looking into before adding send volume.
The plan above is already conservative for this reason (five emails across four weeks), but the
churn number points at something upstream of any single campaign: list hygiene, acquisition
source, or expectations set at signup.
